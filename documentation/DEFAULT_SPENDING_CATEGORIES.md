# Default Spending Categories

## Essential

1. Bills - Rent/mortgage, utilities, maintenance
2. Food—Groceries, dining out
3. Transport—Car payments, gas, insurance, public transit
4. Healthcare—Insurance, medical expenses, prescriptions
5. Animal—Food, vet bills, supplies

## Lifestyle

1. Entertainment - Movies, streaming, hobbies
2. Clothes - Apparel and accessories
3. Beauty - Hygiene, grooming, fitness
4. Home - General purchases, household items
5. Sport - Gym memberships, equipment, activities
6. Cafe - Coffee, tea, snacks, restaurants

## Financial

1. Savings - Emergency fund, retirement
2. Debt - Credit cards, loans
3. Insurance - Life, disability, property
4. Miscellaneous - Unexpected expenses, gifts
